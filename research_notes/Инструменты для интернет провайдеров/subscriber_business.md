## (a) What do subscribers complain about, and why do they churn? (reviews: small vs big ISPs)

### Takeaway
Globally, price is still the reason people most often give for switching. Measured churn intent, though, is driven more and more by quality: reliability, slow speeds, and above all in-home Wi-Fi problems combined with poor support. In Parks/TechSee data, households with Wi-Fi + speed + service problems are about 6x more likely to churn. Russian review sites repeat the same themes: speed below the advertised rate, support that is hard to reach or incompetent, and price rises after a promo ends. In the US, small fiber providers and municipal networks consistently beat cable incumbents on satisfaction.

### Cited Findings
**Churn drivers, measured [GLOBAL]**
- Parks Associates + TechSee white paper "Seeing the Unseen", from a survey of 8,000 US internet households (released 2026, Enterprise Connect). Households with all 3 problems (Wi-Fi dead spots, slow downloads, poor customer service) had an average NPS of −28, and 43% were likely to switch within 12 months. Households with none of these problems had an NPS of +22, and only 7% were likely to switch. That is a 50-point NPS swing. — [Parks Associates](https://www.parksassociates.com/blogs/home-systems-and-controls/parks-associates-and-techsee-research-wi-fi-quality-gaps-drive-50-point-nps-swing-and-put-43-of-at-risk-broadband-households-on-the-brink-of-churn); [Telecompetitor](https://www.telecompetitor.com/in-home-wi-fi-problems-create-customer-churn-risk-parks-associates/)
- Same study: 40% of smart-device owners report losing wireless connectivity as a common issue. 41% of people with device problems need professional support. Only about 7% of US internet households use their ISP's app for support. — [Parks Associates](https://www.parksassociates.com/blogs/home-systems-and-controls/parks-associates-and-techsee-research-wi-fi-quality-gaps-drive-50-point-nps-swing-and-put-43-of-at-risk-broadband-households-on-the-brink-of-churn)
- A 2025 survey says quality of service, not price, drives the wish to switch. Among US respondents considering a switch, 42% name poor internet quality as the main reason. The quality problems cited are devices disconnecting (58%), video freezing (55%) and slow browsing (50%). About 1 in 3 customers in the US and the UK are considering switching. — [Telecoms.com](https://www.telecoms.com/broadband/internet-customers-churn-for-quality-not-price-survey); [Telecompetitor](https://www.telecompetitor.com/quality-issues-drive-internet-churn-survey/)
- 51% of consumers say they would switch if connectivity issues were not resolved quickly. — [Sequential Tech blog](https://www.sequentialtech.com/blog/broadband-subscriber-churn-support-fix/) (vendor blog, original survey not identified)
- Price is still the most-cited switching reason in other surveys: 38% name price as a main reason they would switch. — [LocalCableDeals 2024](https://www.localcabledeals.com/resources/broadband-customer-satisfaction-report-2024); [Allconnect 2025](https://www.allconnect.com/blog/internet-customer-satisfaction-report). These conflict with the "quality not price" finding above. The difference is most likely stated reasons versus reasons among those actively at risk.
- Billing-system vendor Sonar names price first, then reliability, as the most common and "most emotionally charged" churn reason. — [Sonar Software](https://sonar.software/blog/understanding-and-preventing-broadband-churn-why-customers-leave-and-how-isps-can-stop-it)
- UK regulator data on pricing and consumer engagement (switching, out-of-contract price rises) is in the Ofcom 2025 report. — [Ofcom 2025 PDF](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/siteassets/resources/documents/research-and-data/multi-sector/pricing/2025/pricing-and-consumer-engagement-report.pdf?v=413104)

  - Source: [J.D. Power press release](https://www.jdpower.com/business/press-releases/2025-us-residential-internet-service-provider-satisfaction-study/); [GFiber blog](https://gfiber.com/blog/2025/10/three-peat-gfiber-is-1-internet-in-jd.html)
- ACSI 2025: the ISP industry scored 72, up from 71. Fiber averaged 75 vs non-fiber 70, and the gap is narrowing. AT&T Fiber and T-Mobile both scored 78. — [ACSI May 2025 PDF (CPUC docket)](https://docs.cpuc.ca.gov/PublishedDocs/Efile/G000/M591/K819/591819761.PDF). ISPs historically rank near the bottom of all ACSI industries ("beat only gas stations"). — [Light Reading](https://www.lightreading.com/broadband/isps-beat-only-gas-stations-in-acsi-consumer-satisfaction-survey). An ACSI 2026 telecom study exists. — [ACSI 2026 PDF](https://theacsi.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/26may_Telecom-Cell-Smartwatch-Study.pdf)

- Consumer Reports, older (2017) but often cited: Comcast and Spectrum rated low on value and service, while municipal EPB Fiber (Chattanooga) and Google Fiber rated top. — [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2017/06/20/comcast-spectrum-xfinity-consumer-reports) (pre-2020, context only)

  - Sources: [AnandTech forums](https://forums.anandtech.com/threads/frontier-fiber-yay-or-nay.2628835/post-41423368); [Substack "breaking up with spectrum"](https://sweetmercifulzeus.substack.com/p/breaking-up-with-spectrum)

The label can now sit behind a link, and location-based fees can be folded into an "up to" price. — [Streamingmeme](https://www.streamingmeme.com/articles/fcc-broadband-label-rule-changes-allow-isps-to-obscure-fee-details); [Sen. **Russian reviews (otzovik / 2ГИС / Yandex / aggregators) [CIS]**
- Recurring complaints across ISP review aggregators:
  - Real speed far below the tariff, with big fluctuations.
  - "Price doesn't match quality."
  - Support advertised as 24/7 but in practice 10:00–22:00, with waits of up to 20 minutes.
  - Rude or incompetent support staff who don't resolve problems.
  - Price increases after a promo ends.
  - Unexpected disconnections.
  - Positives cited for top-rated ISPs: stable speed and fast installation.
  - Sources: [101internet.ru Уфа reviews](https://101internet.ru/ufa/reviews); [piter-online.net reviews](https://piter-online.net/reviews); [piter-online Билайн](https://piter-online.net/rating/beeline); [dominternet.ru МТС](https://dominternet.ru/msk/providers/mts/reviews/); [Yandex Maps МТС reviews](https://yandex.com/maps/org/mts_moskva_internet_i_televideniye/106454947586/reviews/); [nets.ru Дом.ру](https://nets.ru/reviews/domru)
- Regional ISPs lose customers when they can't match a popular product such as TV/IPTV; customers move to competitors with a wider product range. — [iptvportal.ru](https://iptvportal.ru/startiptv/) (vendor page)

### Inferences
- The strongest measured churn lever for a small ISP is the combined in-home Wi-Fi + support experience, not the access line itself. Proactive Wi-Fi diagnostics and fast resolution directly address the "43% vs 7%" gap.
- Small and municipal fiber providers already win on satisfaction in the US (GFiber, EPB). The usual weakness of small ISPs is support availability and depth: limited hours and few staff. This matches the Russian reviews.
- Russian complaints closely mirror the global ones: speed vs tariff, support, post-promo price hikes.

### Gaps
- I found no aggregated quantitative analysis of otzovik/irecommend/2ГИС reviews comparing small vs large Russian ISPs. Only individual reviews and aggregator pages are available.
- No Trustpilot or reddit dataset with structured small-vs-big comparisons; the evidence is anecdotal.
- No published churn rates for Russian regional ISPs.

## (b) Business and process inefficiencies

### Takeaway
In Russia, the biggest structural pressures on small ISPs are:
- Consolidation by MTS, ЭР-Телеком and Ростелеком.
- Convergent (mobile + ШПД + TV) bundles that small ISPs cannot match: MTS and Билайн hold more than 70% of the convergent market.
- A proposed 2025–26 licensing reform with steep fees, capital requirements and up-front СОРМ, which industry associations say could eliminate local operators in 27 regions.
- Building access via УК and developers, now being liberalized by a 2024 law.

Globally, the main drags are support load from Wi-Fi problems, truck rolls, and high CAC.

**Support and field-ops inefficiency [GLOBAL]**
- 30–50% of all calls to ISP customer service are Wi-Fi related (ASSIA white paper). Omdia interviews put it at up to 60%. 10–30% of such calls end in a truck roll. Cost per Wi-Fi trouble ticket is $22–$471 depending on severity (North America). — [Wi-Fi NOW / ASSIA](https://wifinowglobal.com/news-and-blog/assia-white-paper-30-50-of-customer-services-calls-to-isps-a-result-of-poor-wi-fi/)

- CTC (small US cooperative ISP) cut truck rolls by 50% with a "broadband-in-a-box" self-install plus managed Wi-Fi. — [Calix 2023](https://www.calix.com/blog/2023/04/innovative-broadband-in-a-box-helps-ctc-increase-service-activation-and-cut-truck-rolls-by-50-percent.html)
- Only about 7% of US households use their ISP app for support, which leaves a large self-service gap. — [Parks/TechSee](https://www.parksassociates.com/blogs/home-systems-and-controls/parks-associates-and-techsee-research-wi-fi-quality-gaps-drive-50-point-nps-swing-and-put-43-of-at-risk-broadband-households-on-the-brink-of-churn)
- US competition:
  - Major cable operators lost an estimated 280k broadband subscribers in Q1 2026, an improvement on 320k lost in Q1 2025, as FWA and fiber take share. — [Parks via PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/parks-associates-major-cable-providers-lost-an-estimated-280-000-broadband-subscribers-in-q1-2026--improving-from-an-estimated-320-000-decline-in-q1-2025--302824293.html)
  - 22% of US households get internet and mobile as a bundle. — [Parks at CES](https://www.parksassociates.com/blogs/press-releases/parks-associates-at-ces-22-of-us-households-now-receive-internet-and-mobile-services-as-part-of-a-bundle)

### Inferences
- Small CIS ISPs are squeezed from three sides at once: big operators' convergent bundles (which they cannot match without MVNO partnerships), consolidation buyouts, and regulatory cost (licensing, СОРМ). Being acquired becomes a realistic exit, and tooling that lifts margin and valuation (low churn, clean billing data) matters more.
- The 2024 МКД law shifts competition in existing buildings toward service quality and marketing rather than exclusive access. Agreements with УК may still be friction in practice, since УК still control physical access to technical rooms.
- Wi-Fi-driven support load (30–60% of calls) is the clearest automation target: remote diagnostics, an app, and self-install.

### Gaps
- Connection lead time (заявка → подключение) benchmarks: none found for Russia or globally.
- Receivables / debt (дебиторка) levels, payment-collection practices and the share of prepaid vs postpaid among Russian small ISPs: not found.
- B2B customer economics for small ISPs (share of revenue, SLAs): not found beyond generic B2B ARPU ($200–500/mo, low-quality source in section c).
- Actual enforcement experience with the 2024 МКД law (do УК still demand payment informally?): primary articles were blocked.

### Inferences

- At 1.5–2% monthly churn, about 17–22% of the base turns over each year. Even small churn reductions are worth a lot relative to CAC.

## (d) What customers wish ISPs offered, and which value-added services sell

### Takeaway
Globally:
- Managed / whole-home Wi-Fi and gateway-based cybersecurity are the value-added services that sell best.
- Parental controls matter to about half of subscribers (Calix).
- Security has influenced switching for more than a third of people who switched.
- App-based self-service is under-used, at about 7%.

In Russia, regional ISPs build "smart building" bundles: IPTV, видеонаблюдение, smart intercoms (умный домофон) with Face ID, СКУД, and telemetry. The lack of TV/IPTV is a cited churn cause.

### Cited Findings
- Calix Market Insights:
  - 53% of subscribers say parental controls are important.
  - 37% use home internet for work.
  - More than 1 in 3 who switched providers chose security services.
  - Sources: [Calix subscriber survey](https://www.calix.com/forms/marketer/download/subscriber-survey-results.html); [BusinessWire 2023](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20230801020242/en)

### Inferences
- In Russia, the МКД-anchored set of домофон + видеонаблюдение + СКУД is the regional ISP's natural counter to big operators' mobile bundles. It ties the ISP to the building or УК relationship, which a mobile operator cannot easily replicate.
- A mobile app with outage status, speed checks and Wi-Fi diagnostics addresses several complaint categories at once (support reachability, "real speed", Wi-Fi). Low app usage (about 7%) suggests current apps don't deliver this.
