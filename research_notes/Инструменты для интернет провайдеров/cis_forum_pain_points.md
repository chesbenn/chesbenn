## 1. What do ISP engineers and owners complain about most (forums, habr, nag.ru, Telegram, vc.ru)?

### Takeaway
In 2022–2026 the loudest complaints from small ISPs are not technical. They are about **regulation driving costs up and squeezing them out of the market** (СОРМ, Яровая, ТСПУ, the 2026 Минцифры licensing reform), **staff shortages (especially installers)**, **YouTube throttling and ТСПУ side effects that drive customers away**, and **DDoS/cyberattacks**. Day-to-day technical complaints on forums cluster around PON ONU drop-offs, Mikrotik BRAS CPU load, CGNAT/IPv4, cheap customer routers generating support calls, and building access.

### Cited Findings

**A. Regulation and market-exit pressure (very frequent, the dominant 2023–2026 theme)**
- Minimum СОРМ cost for a small regional ISP is **5–15 M RUB**, and over **30 M RUB** for high-bandwidth networks. Software СОРМ alone costs 5–10 M RUB, with hardware on top. For a small operator with **10–20 M RUB annual revenue** this eats the whole operating profit, and in effect "deprives small companies of the chance for legal market entry". Operators used legal loopholes for years to delay installing it. — [Rambler/finance "Убить дракона"](https://finance.rambler.ru/business/56986157-ubit-drakona-kak-telekom-rynok-vykormil-zverya-s-kotorym-teper-ne-mozhet-spravitsya/); [dcc.su "Сколько стоит купить СОРМ"](https://dcc.su/articles/sorm-stoimost-cena/)
- nag.ru ran a practitioner guide on how to defend yourself against demands to buy СОРМ under the Yarovaya law. There is also a forum.nag.ru thread on Rostelecom's "аутСОРМ" (СОРМ as a service), which small ISPs discuss as a way around buying hardware. — [nag.ru/material/36241](https://nag.ru/material/36241); [forum.nag.ru аутСОРМ от Ростелеком](https://forum.nag.ru/index.php?%2Ftopic%2F196984-autsorm-ot-rostelekom%2F=); [Rostelecom B2O СОРМ service](https://msk.rt.ru/b2o/services-date/copm)
- Yarovaya requires storing message content for up to 6 months, metadata for 3 years and internet traffic for 30 days. Critics (ComNews/AOTC) say it "violates fair competition": the cost of storage per subscriber falls with scale, which pushes the market toward oligopoly. — [TAdviser: Закон Яровой](https://www.tadviser.ru/index.php/%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D1%8F:%D0%97%D0%B0%D0%BA%D0%BE%D0%BD_%D0%AF%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B9_(%D0%9E_%D0%B2%D0%BD%D0%B5%D1%81%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B8_%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%BC%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D0%B2_%D0%A3%D0%9A_%D0%B8_%D0%A3%D0%9F%D0%9A_%D0%A0%D0%A4_%D0%B2_%D1%87%D0%B0%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B8_%D1%83%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B0%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%8F_%D0%B4%D0%BE%D0%BF%D0%BE%D0%BB%D0%BD%D0%B8%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BB%D1%8C%D0%BD%D1%8B%D1%85_%D0%BC%D0%B5%D1%80_%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%BE%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%B2%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%B5%D0%B9%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%8F_%D1%82%D0%B5%D1%80%D1%80%D0%BE%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%B7%D0%BC%D1%83)); [ComNews "Подводные камни закона Яровой" (2018)](https://www.comnews.ru/content/115129/2018-10-01/podvodnye-kamni-zakona-yarovoy); [AOTC copy](http://aotc.su/sobesednik/podvodnye-kamni-zakona-yarovoj.html); [ComNews "Закон Яровой: работа над ошибками" (2022)](https://www.comnews.ru/content/219525/2022-03-31/2022-w13/zakon-yarovoy-rabota-nad-oshibkami)
- **Минцифры telecom reform (2026):** the proposal includes three new licence types costing **1–50 M RUB**, minimum charter capital of **5–100 M RUB**, no licences for individual entrepreneurs (ИП), licence revocation for repeated violations with a 10-year ban for beneficiaries, and lifting the moratorium on planned inspections, which currently runs to 2030. AMOR complained to Putin, Mishustin and the Prosecutor General, arguing the reform would leave only state-controlled operators. According to AMOR, small operators (revenue under 50 M RUB) employ about **15,200** people and medium ones (50–500 M RUB) about **21,800**. A regional ISP owner warned that tariffs could become "даже самой неприличной" once competition disappears. — [iStories, 14 Apr 2026](https://storage.googleapis.com/istories/news/2026/04/14/provaideri-predupredili-chto-novie-initsiativi-mintsifri-podnimut-stoimost-tarifov-na-internet-i-voobshche-lishat-dostupa-v-set-zhitelei-nekotorikh-raionov-rossii/index.html)
- The head of the operators' association (АСТО) estimated the market could shrink from **about 5,000 active operators to about 1,500**, hitting rural areas hardest, where the small ISP is often the only provider. The industry headline was "up to 70% of operators may leave the market". — [Cableman.ru](https://www.cableman.ru/content/do-70-operatorov-mogut-uiti-s-rynka-otraslevye-assotsiatsii-raskritikovali-initsiativu) (snippet only; fetch blocked)
- nag.ru has an article titled "Оператор, которого нет". From the search snippet context it appears to be about regulatory and СОРМ status for small operators, but the content could not be verified. — [nag.ru/material/43137](https://nag.ru/material/43137)

**B. ТСПУ (DPI boxes owned by Roskomnadzor) and blocking (very frequent since 2021)**
- ComNews (Jun 2023), "Operators turned out to be the ones blamed for non-working ТСПУ": operators pay for the electricity at ТСПУ sites and rent channels of at least 100 Mbit/s to connect ТСПУ to the central system. Because ТСПУ blocking is ineffective, regulators then also require operators to block resources themselves, which duplicates the work. — [ComNews 26.06.2023](https://www.comnews.ru/content/227012/2023-06-26/2023-w26/operatory-okazalis-kraynimi-za-nerabotayuschikh-tspu-roskomnadzora); [Forbes.ru: Минцифры заставляет операторов самих блокировать](https://www.forbes.ru/tekhnologii/491375-strogo-po-trafiku-mincifry-zastavlaet-operatorov-samih-blokirovat-veb-resursy)
- Operators are required to submit a diagram of how traffic passes through ТСПУ (network maps). Small operators suspect this exists mainly so that large networks can expose data about small ones. — [Roskomsvoboda](https://roskomsvoboda.org/ru/post/shema-propuska-trafika-cherez-tspu/); [ComNews "ТСПУ все ближе" 2021](https://www.comnews.ru/content/216028/2021-08-19/2021-w33/tspu-vse-blizhe)
- Practitioner-visible effects: higher ping, dropped video calls, page timeouts and VPN breakage. Subscribers blame the ISP. — [SecurityLab blog](https://www.securitylab.ru/blog/personal/Neurosinaps/360711.php); [Pikabu "ТСПУ у провайдеров: коротко и по фактам"](https://pikabu.ru/story/tspu_u_provayderov_korotko_i_po_faktam_13757014)
- **YouTube throttling, Aug 2024:** AMOR (120+ members) wrote to Минцифры and RKN that the slowdown hit operators "sharply and chaotically" across regions and that they were "massively losing their subscriber base". More than 1,000 subscribers cancelled within two weeks, and more than 135 operators were affected. Mobile networks had no problem at the time, so subscribers blamed their home ISP. ISPs have no access to the ТСПУ boxes. — [Habr news 835356](https://habr.com/ru/news/835356/); [Kommersant 6890897](https://www.kommersant.ru/doc/6890897); [CNews](https://www.cnews.ru/news/top/2024-08-12_neozhidannye_posledstviya); [RBC](https://www.rbc.ru/technology_and_media/12/08/2024/66b99fe09a7947044dca8d2d); [Xakep](https://xakep.ru/2024/08/12/youtube-operators-letter/); [Forbes.ru](https://www.forbes.ru/tekhnologii/518863-rossiane-nacali-razryvat-dogovory-s-regional-nymi-operatorami-svazi-iz-za-youtube)

**E. Building access (МКД) and management companies (frequent, long-running)**
- Management companies (УК) and homeowners' associations (ТСЖ) historically controlled access to apartment buildings and charged ISPs fees. Since **April 2024** the rules give free access without a residents' meeting decision and no fee for using common property (electricity is still paid). ### Inferences
- The ranking of pain by owners in 2024–2026 is roughly: (1) regulatory capex and opex (СОРМ, ТСПУ, the licensing reform) as an existential threat; (2) lack of installers; (3) customers lost for reasons outside the ISP's control (YouTube/ТСПУ, competition from bundled mobile offers); (4) DDoS and security; (5) building access; (6) rising equipment cost. Purely technical forum complaints (section 4) are constant background noise rather than headline issues.
- A recurring meta-pain is that **the ISP is blamed for things it cannot see or control** (ТСПУ boxes, YouTube, the customer's own router). Tools that prove "it's not our network" to the subscriber and support staff would address real demand.

## 2. Regulatory burdens (СОРМ, Яровая, РКН registry/blocking, ТСПУ, licences) and how they hurt small ISPs

### Takeaway
Regulation is the #1 owner-level pain. It is a fixed cost that does not scale with subscriber count, so a 2,000-subscriber ISP pays nearly as much as a 50,000-subscriber one. On top of that come recurring opex (ТСПУ power and channels, blocklist upkeep, СОРМ maintenance), legal risk (licence revocation, inspections from 2030 or earlier under the 2026 reform), and lost customers from ТСПУ side effects.

### Cited Findings
- СОРМ capex is 5–15 M RUB for a small ISP, against revenue of 10–20 M RUB per year. — [Rambler](https://finance.rambler.ru/business/56986157-ubit-drakona-kak-telekom-rynok-vykormil-zverya-s-kotorym-teper-ne-mozhet-spravitsya/); [dcc.su](https://dcc.su/articles/sorm-stoimost-cena/)
- Outsourced СОРМ from Rostelecom ("аутСОРМ") is discussed as a cheaper path, but it makes the ISP dependent on its largest competitor. — [forum.nag.ru](https://forum.nag.ru/index.php?%2Ftopic%2F196984-autsorm-ot-rostelekom%2F=); [rt.ru B2O](https://msk.rt.ru/b2o/services-date/copm)
- СОРМ requirements are tightening: storage of security logs and administrator action logs. — [TAdviser СОРМ](https://www.tadviser.ru/index.php/%D0%A1%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C%D1%8F:%D0%A1%D0%9E%D0%A0%D0%9C_(%D0%A1%D0%B8%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B5%D0%BC%D0%B0_%D0%BE%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%B2%D0%BD%D0%BE-%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B7%D1%8B%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%BD%D1%8B%D1%85_%D0%BC%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%B8%D1%8F%D1%82%D0%B8%D0%B9))
- СОРМ costs are also pushing up hosting prices from 2025 (an adjacent market). — [servernews](https://servernews.ru/1115817); [CNews market](https://market.cnews.ru/news/top/2024-12-23_v_rossii_dorozhaet_sam_internet?p=homecnews); [org.ru](https://org.ru/news/detail/?article=hosting-dorozhaet-sorm-i-nalogi-povyshayut-stoimos)
- ТСПУ: the operator pays power and a channel of at least 100 Mbit/s, must still block resources itself, and must file a traffic-path diagram. — [ComNews 2023](https://www.comnews.ru/content/227012/2023-06-26/2023-w26/operatory-okazalis-kraynimi-za-nerabotayuschikh-tspu-roskomnadzora); [Roskomsvoboda](https://roskomsvoboda.org/ru/post/shema-propuska-trafika-cherez-tspu/); [Forbes.ru](https://www.forbes.ru/tekhnologii/491375-strogo-po-trafiku-mincifry-zastavlaet-operatorov-samih-blokirovat-veb-resursy)
- ТСПУ also drives churn: more than 1,000 cancellations in 2 weeks across more than 135 operators (Aug 2024). — [Habr](https://habr.com/ru/news/835356/)
- 2026 licensing reform: licence fees of 1–50 M RUB, charter capital of 5–100 M RUB, no ИП, revocation with a 10-year ban, inspections resumed. Industry projects a fall from about 5,000 to about 1,500 operators. — [iStories](https://storage.googleapis.com/istories/news/2026/04/14/provaideri-predupredili-chto-novie-initsiativi-mintsifri-podnimut-stoimost-tarifov-na-internet-i-voobshche-lishat-dostupa-v-set-zhitelei-nekotorikh-raionov-rossii/index.html); [Cableman](https://www.cableman.ru/content/do-70-operatorov-mogut-uiti-s-rynka-otraslevye-assotsiatsii-raskritikovali-initsiativu)
- Getting a licence: Roskomnadzor reviews applications in about 45 days, and separate licences are needed for data transmission and for telematics. — [Carbon Soft "Как стать провайдером"](https://www.carbonsoft.ru/%D0%BA%D0%B0%D0%BA-%D1%81%D1%82%D0%B0%D1%82%D1%8C-%D0%B8%D0%BD%D1%82%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%BD%D0%B5%D1%82-%D0%BF%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%B9%D0%B4%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%BE%D0%BC/)

### Inferences
- Compliance paperwork is labour-intensive for an ISP with 3–10 staff: subscriber data for СОРМ, ТСПУ network diagrams, blocklist handling, licence reporting. Automating compliance data (accurate subscriber, IP and address records exported in the required formats) is a tooling opportunity, but it needs certified vendors for the СОРМ parts.
- The regulatory trend favours consolidation, so any tool vendor that targets small Russian ISPs is selling into a shrinking segment. That is a market risk worth flagging.

## 3. Problems with billing systems (UTM5, LANBilling, Hydra, Abills, Userside, Carbon, home-grown)

### Takeaway
Little specific billing-complaint material came through search snippets, a gap caused by fetches being blocked. What did surface: vendor lifecycle and migration pain (NetUP UTM5 versions dropping support, PostgreSQL support removed), systems chosen for about 1,000 users that don't scale, and the catastrophic case where a cyberattack took billing down for more than a month.

### Cited Findings
- NetUP UTM5: version 5.3-005 dropped from support, 5.3-006 LTS ended by 31 Dec 2020, and PostgreSQL support was removed from 5.3-005, forcing migration to MySQL before upgrading. UTM 5+ was announced in 2019 as the successor. — [netup.ru update page](https://www.netup.ru/ru/utm5/update); [UTM 5+ announcement 2019](https://www.netup.ru/ru/news/year2019/utm5plus); [NetUP support](https://www.netup.tv/ru/utm5/support)
- Habr/LOR discussions: small ISPs pick billing sized for about 1,000 users and then outgrow it. A village ISP adapted the open-source Neon billing as it grew. A long-running linux.org.ru thread asks for "billing for a small provider" (older, 2009-era). — [Habr search results summary incl. habr.com/ru/articles/256485](https://habr.com/ru/articles/256485/); [linux.org.ru 4111587](https://www.linux.org.ru/forum/admin/4111587)
- One attacked ISP took more than a month to restore its site and billing, served subscribers for free and could not collect payments. — [Anti-Malware snippet](https://www.anti-malware.ru/analytics/Threats_Analysis/Cyberattacks-on-Russian-Internet-providers)
- Billing catalogue (historical, 2015) listing many RU billing systems, showing how fragmented the market is. — [dtulyakov catalogue](https://blog.dtulyakov.ru/2015/01/katalog-billingovykh-sistem.html)
- Habr post 444274, "Развёрнутый ответ на комментарий, а также немного о жизни провайдеров в РФ" (2019), is a practitioner account of ISP life. Its content could not be fetched. — [habr.com/ru/post/444274](https://habr.com/ru/post/444274/)

### Inferences
- Likely pain points, consistent with the above but **not directly confirmed** here: billing outages stopping payments and auto-blocking, poor backup and disaster recovery, vendor lock-in and costly migrations, weak integration between billing, the OSS (Userside) and network equipment.

### Gaps
- No snippet-level evidence was found for LANBilling, Hydra, Abills or Carbon Billing complaints. A follow-up should read forum.nag.ru sub-forums "Биллинговые системы" directly from an unblocked network.

## 4. Problems with PON, switches, Mikrotik BRAS, CGNAT/IPv4

### Takeaway
On the forums (forum.nag.ru, local.com.ua, forummikrotik.ru), the most common technical threads are: ONUs dropping off BDCOM/C-Data OLTs, especially with mixed ONU brands ("зоопарк"), fixed by firmware or by finding optical faults; Mikrotik PPPoE BRAS hitting 100% CPU and dropping sessions; CGNAT causing support tickets (cameras, games, IP-based bans); and IPv4 scarcity and cost.

### Cited Findings
- forum.nag.ru keeps a long-running FAQ thread "RU.PON (FAQ о ПОН на BDCOM и альтернативных ONU)", which shows sustained demand for help with BDCOM plus third-party ONUs. — [forum.nag.ru topic 110590](https://forum.nag.ru/index.php?/topic/110590-rupon-faq-o-pon-na-bdcom-i-alternativnyh-onu/)
- local.com.ua (Ukrainian ISP forum):
  - "BDCOM P3110B, периодически отваливаются ONU (зоопарк)": intermittent ONU loss with a mix of ONU brands.
  - "Отвал ONU": dereg reason `llid-admin-down`, where power-cycling the ONU helps temporarily. Support says it usually means a problem with the optical line or the ONU transmitter.
  - P3310B: ONUs dropping every minute after an OLT reboot, fixed by firmware 10.1.0B Build 29333.

  Sources: [local.com.ua 107978](https://local.com.ua/forum/topic/107978-bdcom-p3110b-%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BE%D0%B4%D0%B8%D1%87%D0%B5%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8-%D0%BE%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%B8%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%8E%D1%82%D1%81%D1%8F-onu-%D0%B7%D0%BE%D0%BE%D0%BF%D0%B0%D1%80%D0%BA/); [local.com.ua 101395](https://local.com.ua/forum/topic/101395-%D0%BE%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BB-onu/); [local.com.ua 89192](https://local.com.ua/forum/topic/89192-bdcom-p3310b-%D0%B8-%D0%BE%D1%82%D0%B2%D0%B0%D0%BB%D0%B8%D0%B2%D0%B0%D1%8E%D1%89%D0%B8%D0%B5%D1%81%D1%8F-%D0%BE%D0%BD%D1%83%D1%88%D0%BA%D0%B8/)
- Trade-off: BDCOM OLTs cost much more than C-Data, but C-Data lacks explicit MVR (multicast VLAN registration, needed for IPTV) on its ONUs. — same search set ([forum.nag.ru 110590](https://forum.nag.ru/index.php?/topic/110590-rupon-faq-o-pon-na-bdcom-i-alternativnyh-onu/); [qna.habr.com/q/685647](https://qna.habr.com/q/685647))
- Mikrotik PPPoE: high CPU drops subscribers. Common causes are MPPE encryption and compression enabled, and huge numbers of TCP connections. Threads on forummikrotik.ru and mikrotik.by: "ЦПУ 100% – PPPoE пропадает", "Постоянный обрыв PPPoE". — [forum.mikrotik.by t=2136](https://forum.mikrotik.by/viewtopic.php?t=2136); [forummikrotik.ru t=6772](https://forummikrotik.ru/viewtopic.php?t=6772&start=10); [forummikrotik.ru t=8712](https://forummikrotik.ru/viewtopic.php?t=8712); [local.com.ua 60998 "Mikrotik загрузка CPU 100%"](https://local.com.ua/forum/topic/60998-mikrotik-%D0%B7%D0%B0%D0%B3%D1%80%D1%83%D0%B7%D0%BA%D0%B0-cpu-100/); [mikrotik-wiki PPPoE для провайдера](https://mikrotik-wiki.ru/article/pppoe-server); [serveradmin.ru Mikrotik mistakes](https://serveradmin.ru/perevod-prezentaczii-po-mikrotik-my-holy-war-against-masquerade/)
- CGNAT/IPv4: new ISPs can no longer get IPv4 blocks. Under CGNAT subscribers cannot reach home devices (e.g. CCTV), and sites may ban a shared IP as if it were a DDoS. A public IP per subscriber is "сомнительная затея, да и недешёвая" ("a dubious idea, and not a cheap one"). Subscribers keep asking on Q&A why they can't get a dynamic public IP, or why their "white IP" only works inside the ISP's network. — [Habr VAS Experts (IPv4 shortage)](https://habr.com/ru/companies/vasexperts/articles/316272/); [qna.habr 1068076](https://qna.habr.com/q/1068076); [qna.habr 1377180](https://qna.habr.com/q/1377180); [y-ss.ru CGNAT & CCTV](https://y-ss.ru/blog_pro/videonablyudenie/cgnat-i-videonablyudenie-problemy-s-dostupom-i-sposoby-obkhoda-isp/)
- Customer routers: one claim (comptek on Habr) is that **80%** of speed and stability problems come from a SOHO router the subscriber installed themselves, or from poor quality control of routers the ISP bought. Cheap routers freeze under load. This is an older post (about 2011) but the theme persists, and a Kazakhstan expert in 2025 also blamed "старые роутеры" (old routers). — [Habr comptek 117250](https://habr.com/ru/companies/comptek/articles/117250/) (older); [digitalbusiness.kz 2025](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2025-01-29/meshayut-starie-routeri-i-otsutstvie-optovolokna-ekspert-rasskazal-o-kachestve-interneta-v-kazahstane/)

### Inferences
- Tools that fail here, as the threads imply: vendor OLT web UIs and CLIs (no fleet-wide view of ONU signal and dereg reasons across mixed brands); generic monitoring (Zabbix and the like) that needs per-vendor SNMP templates; and no TR-069/ACS for cheap routers, which leaves support blind to the customer's Wi-Fi.
- The way Ukraine's market moved to PON during the blackouts shows PON operations tooling growing in importance across the CIS.

### Gaps
- Not verified within budget: specific Eltex/SNR switch bug complaints, V-Sol, Huawei/ZTE licensing issues, Mikrotik CHR or accel-ppp comparisons.

## 5. Staff and process issues (support, installers, inventory (ТМЦ), cable journal and documentation)

### Takeaway
The staff problem is mainly about **availability** (no installers on the market, rising wages), with process problems layered on top: tracking equipment issued to installers, documenting the fibre plant (splices, splitters, ports), and handling support calls that are mostly about customer-side Wi-Fi or ТСПУ effects.

### Cited Findings
- Installer shortage and wage race: see §1C. — [Habr 1009810](https://habr.com/ru/news/1009810/); [nag.ru 43145](https://nag.ru/material/43145)
- ТМЦ (inventory) and work orders: Userside (the de-facto Russian ISP OSS) has modules for inventory, automatic movement of items into a task and back to the warehouse, and document numbers and dates. Its vendor answers many forum.nag.ru threads about these features, which shows active demand and gaps that users raise. — [Userside wiki ТМЦ](https://wiki.userside.eu/%D0%A2%D0%9C%D0%A6); [Userside wiki Операции](https://wiki.userside.eu/%D0%9E%D0%BF%D0%B5%D1%80%D0%B0%D1%86%D0%B8%D0%B8); [forum.nag.ru UserSide content](https://forum.nag.ru/index.php?%2Fprofile%2F54301-userside%2F=&change_section=1&do=content&type=forums_topic_post); [Exler on UserSide](https://exler.livejournal.com/3826226.html)
- Fibre plant documentation: alternatives in use include Userside (splice wizard, QR labels), MoBill-Cross, UniCableMap, Naumen Cable Management and online configurators. A Habr Q&A, "Где найти программу для работы со схемами оптоволокна?", shows small ISPs searching for one. — [qna.habr 136757](https://qna.habr.com/q/136757); [MoBill-Cross](https://mobill.ru/products/mobill-cross.html); [UniCableMap](http://www.resident.ru/software/unicablemap/default.htm); [Naumen](https://www.naumen.ru/products/inventory/functions/cable-management/); [vols.expert](https://vols.expert/configurator/)
- Support load: practitioners and vendors repeatedly say that most "internet doesn't work" calls trace to the customer's router or Wi-Fi (see §4). Remote router management with a 15-minute resolution target is described as best practice. Rostelecom support's work is described on nag.ru. — [Habr comptek](https://habr.com/ru/companies/comptek/articles/117250/); [nag.ru 26515](https://nag.ru/material/26515); [RIA](https://ria.ru/20130930/966674329.html)

### Inferences
- With experienced installers scarce, tools that let **inexperienced** installers work correctly would address the top staffing pain: a mobile app with step-by-step tasks, ONU activation without calling the NOC, photo-based splice documentation, automatic ТМЦ write-off.
- The fibre documentation market is fragmented (Userside dominates small ISPs; Naumen and MoBill serve larger ones). Excel and paper cable journals are presumably still common in small ISPs, but I found no direct evidence for this.

### Gaps
- No direct practitioner quotes on installer theft or loss of ТМЦ, overtime, or support-agent burnout.
- No survey data on what share of small ISPs use Userside, Excel or paper for documentation.
